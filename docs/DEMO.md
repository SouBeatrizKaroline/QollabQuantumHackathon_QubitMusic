# Public demo video script — approximately 2 minutes 30 seconds

Record the Qollab application once its simulator execution is verified. The final video must be public and require no sign-in. This script is ready to record; no video has been generated or published by this repository.

**0:00–0:20 — A concrete need**

“I am making a short lesson and need a gentle 24-second instrumental cue. Finding a recording, reviewing its license, and trimming it adds work. QubitMusic turns a simple creative brief into synthesized audio.”

**0:20–0:50 — Generate and listen**

Show the Qollab page. Run on the free built-in simulator. Choose the lesson preset, duration and warm keys. Generate, then play a few seconds. Point out the score, six sections and export WAV button.

**0:50–1:25 — Show the meaningful quantum contribution**

“Six qubits choose gentle or energetic phrases. Our quadratic cost balances energy fit, smoother transitions and gentle endpoints. Classical code tunes a one-layer QAOA circuit. The selected backend measures it, and we synthesize the best observed arrangement.”

Expand the explanation and show shot counts, gamma/beta, selected cost, exact optimum and gap. Mention that the browser preview is labelled as simulation and the Qollab entry uses Qiskit.

**1:25–1:50 — Be honest about the baseline**

Click Hear classical optimum.

“We compare against exact enumeration on the same objective. With only 64 arrangements, classical enumeration is cheaper. This prototype does not claim quantum advantage; it makes a hybrid optimization experiment useful and understandable.”

**1:50–2:10 — Accessibility and usable outputs**

Switch to pt-BR, pt-PT and Spanish. Export WAV and show the duration. Export project JSON for note-level reproducibility. No account inside the app or audio uploads are needed.

**2:10–2:30 — Quantum Impact and next steps**

“Our intended impact is lowering the effort of soundtrack creation for educators and small creators. We have not yet measured user outcomes. Next we will evaluate time-to-usable-cue, compare against classical heuristics at larger sizes, and test noisy simulators. The code is MIT licensed and the project is runnable on Qollab.”

Only say the last phrase after publication and execution are verified. If an IonQ simulator run was completed, show its recorded result rather than claiming hardware execution.
